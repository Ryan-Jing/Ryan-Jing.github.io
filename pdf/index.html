VAD Keyword Detector

Classical speech-processing keyword detector without AI keyword models

Python, Signal Processing, Speech Processing, MFCC, DTW

Built a keyword detection application that compares live audio against a calibrated keyword template using signal-processing features instead of an AI classifier.

  • Calibration begins with a three-second ambient noise sample to estimate energy and spectral entropy thresholds.
  • Preprocessing removes DC offset, applies an 80 Hz to 7 kHz Butterworth bandpass filter, and adds pre-emphasis.
  • Keyword recordings are framed into 25 ms windows with 10 ms hops, cropped by a custom VAD, and converted into MFCC features.
  • Feature extraction uses a Hamming window, 512-point FFT, 40 mel filters, log mel spectrum, type-II DCT, 13 MFCC coefficients, and first/second-order deltas for 39 features per frame.
  • Live monitoring stores 16 kHz microphone audio in a three-second ring buffer and uses speech, hangover, and silence states to avoid choppy segmentation.
  • Dynamic time warping compares live segments against the template with Euclidean frame distances and a Sakoe-Chiba band constrained to about 20 percent of sequence length.
  • The normalized DTW score is compared against a threshold of 3.0; performance is strongest with clean calibration and consistent pitch, tone, and pacing.
